# Executive Summary
## Customer Segmentation & Churn Pattern Analytics in European Banking

### Purpose
This project examines customer churn patterns across a public practice dataset representing customers in France, Germany and Spain. It focuses on the distribution of churn across geography, age, gender, credit score, tenure, account balance, product holdings and estimated salary.

### What the dashboard provides
- Overall customer count, churned customer count and churn rate.
- Geography-wise and age-wise churn comparisons.
- Gender, tenure and credit-score segment comparisons.
- Churn patterns by balance segment and number of products.
- Financial profile comparisons for churned and retained customers.
- Interactive filters and CSV export for further review.

### Decision-use
The dashboard is intended to help analysts identify segments for further investigation. Higher observed churn in a segment should prompt review of customer feedback, service quality, engagement and product fit. Retention actions should be tested through measurable pilots.

### Important qualification
The default dataset is a public practice dataset, not verified records from the European Central Bank or a named bank. Results are illustrative and must not be presented as official institutional findings. A final stakeholder version should be refreshed with approved, representative data and reviewed for privacy and fairness.
